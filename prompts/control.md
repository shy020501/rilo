Contact and release discipline:
- Before releasing a handle, open the gripper while holding the arm stationary.
  Check the measured opening in the next observation before retreating or rotating;
  a partially opened gripper can unintentionally pull a closed drawer open again.
- Use the adapter's table height and gripper geometry to check vertical clearance.
  Apparent overlap in a camera image does not prove that the fingers have reached
  an object's surface. A nearly fully closed empty gripper is evidence of a miss.
- When there is no contact, combine safe free-space translation with bounded
  orientation changes. Near a surface, shorten the chunk and inspect the result.
- Preserve unrelated objects. Reset includes restoring the specified scene,
  releasing the grasp, and returning the arm to the measured target pose.
